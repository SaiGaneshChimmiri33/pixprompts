How to use the prompts on PixPrompts

A friendly walkthrough from picking a prompt to getting a result you are happy with, including what happens to your photos.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

PixPrompts is a free library of copy-ready prompts for editing photos with AI tools. We built it so you do not need to be an expert at writing prompts. You pick one, copy it and paste it into the AI tool you already use. This guide shows the whole process, with a few tips that make the results better.

What PixPrompts is, and what it is not

PixPrompts is a collection of text prompts. It is not an image editor and we do not process your photos. You never upload a picture to our site. All the editing happens in the AI tool you choose, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney or Meta AI. We are an independent website and are not affiliated with any of these providers.

Step 1: Find a prompt you like

On the home page, browse the categories or use the search box with a word such as portrait, vintage, saree, couple or cinematic. Each card shows the title, the prompt text and the tools it works with. Read the prompt before you copy it. If it describes a style that you do not like, move on, because there are plenty of others.

Step 2: Copy the prompt

Tap the Copy button on the card. The prompt is placed on your clipboard, including the final line that asks the AI to keep your face and the photo’s original details unchanged. Nothing is sent anywhere. The copy happens on your own device.

Step 3: Choose a good photo

This is the part that people skip, and it matters most. Pick a sharp, evenly lit photo where your face is visible. Use the original file and not a screenshot. If you want tips, our guide on preparing your photo takes only a few minutes to read.

Step 4: Open your AI tool and paste

Open the AI app or website you prefer, upload your photo and paste the prompt into the message box. If the tool asks what you want to do with the photo, choose the option for editing or changing it. Then send it and wait for the result. Different tools take different amounts of time.

Step 5: Review the result honestly

Put the original and the result side by side. Does it look like you? Are the outfit, hair and background what you expected? Do the hands and edges look natural? If yes, great. If not, move to the next step instead of giving up.

Step 6: Adjust with one small change

Add a short instruction to your message and try again. Some examples that work well:

  • “Make the colours more natural and less saturated.”
  • “Keep my original hairstyle.”
  • “Use softer lighting with natural skin texture.”
  • “Keep the background simpler.”

Change only one thing each time so you can see what helped. Our guide on fixing common AI photo editing mistakes lists many more fixes.

Step 7: Save what works

When you get a result you love, save the prompt and your adjustments in a notes app. Over time, you will build your own collection and learn which wording suits which kind of photo.

Tips for better results

  • Try the same prompt in two tools. They often give very different looks.
  • Run the prompt more than once. Each attempt is slightly different.
  • Do not stack many big changes at once. Edit in two steps if needed.
  • Mix and match. You can take the lighting from one prompt and the setting from another.

Using AI tools responsibly

Please only edit photos that you own or have permission to use. Do not edit pictures of other people without asking them, and never create images that could mislead, embarrass or harm someone. Respect the rules of the AI tool that you use. You can read more in our Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Disclaimer.

About ads and affiliate links

PixPrompts is free to use. The site may show ads and may contain affiliate links, which can earn us a small commission at no extra cost to you. We explain this on our affiliate disclosure page. We do not let ads or commissions decide which prompts we publish.

Need help or have a request?

If a prompt does not work as expected, or you would like a prompt for a particular style or occasion, write to us from the contact page. We read every message, and your ideas often turn into new prompts.

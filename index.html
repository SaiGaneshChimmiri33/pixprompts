FAQ

Quick answers.

Are these prompts free?

Yes. Every prompt on PixPrompts is free to copy and use. Check your AI tool’s terms for how you can use the images it creates.

Which AI tools do they work with?

Each card lists the tools we tested it with, such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney and Meta AI. Results vary by tool, so adjust one detail at a time.

Why does Copy add a “keep my face accurate” line?

When the checkbox is on, Copy adds one short line telling the AI to keep your face, features and photo details unchanged. Turn it off to write your own.

Do I upload my photo to PixPrompts?

No. You copy the prompt here and paste it into your AI tool together with your own photo. We never receive your images.

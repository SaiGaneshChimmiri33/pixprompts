About

Better AI photo edits start with better prompts.

PixPrompts is a free, independent library of copy-ready prompts for editing photos with AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney and Meta AI.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Why it exists

Getting a good AI photo edit usually comes down to wording. A vague request gives a vague result. PixPrompts collects tested, trending looks and writes them as clear prompts, so you can tap Copy, upload your photo and paste, without trial and error.

How it works

Pick a style or search by keyword, copy the prompt, then paste it into your AI tool along with your own photo. Each prompt says what to change and what to keep, such as the face, pose and clothing. We never ask you to upload photos to this site.

How prompts are made

Each prompt is written and reviewed by hand, tried in more than one tool where possible, and refined when results look off. Results still vary by tool and by photo, so we include tips for adjusting one detail at a time.

Responsible use

Only edit photos you have the right to use, and never edit other people’s photos without permission. See our Terms of Use.

Supporting the site

PixPrompts is free. It is supported by ads and affiliate links, explained in our Affiliate Disclosure and Privacy Policy.

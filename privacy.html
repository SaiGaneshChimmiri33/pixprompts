Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PixPrompts (pixprompts.com) is a library of copy-ready AI photo editing prompts. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not require an account, and we do not ask you to upload photos. You never upload images to PixPrompts; prompts are copied and pasted into third-party AI tools. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Like most sites, our hosting provider and analytics tools may automatically collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed and referring page.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your browsing history.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings or learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we show a consent banner and only use personalized ads or non-essential cookies with your consent.

Affiliate links

Some links lead to third-party products and may earn us a commission. Those third parties have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies once you visit them. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

How we use information

We use data to operate and improve the site, understand which prompts are useful, show ads, and answer messages. We do not sell your personal information.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold, or object to its processing. Email us and we will respond.

Children

PixPrompts is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
